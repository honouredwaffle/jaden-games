🎣 How To Fish

1
Watch the water — fish swim below the boat. Bigger + brighter = rarer.
2
THROW: hold the THROW button to charge the bar, release to cast. More power = deeper + further.
3
Wait: a fish swims over and starts nibbling — the float dips. Usually best to WAIT, it circles and taps up to 3 times. Reel too early and it'll spook (sometimes it just backs off and stays).
4
Hook it: when it finally commits the bobber slams right under — TAP to set the hook.
5
REEL: HOLD the REEL button to pull the fish up — the catch bar rises toward the surface. It'll thrash and drag the bar back down. Release before the tension bar snaps.
6
Bar full = caught. Bar empty = it snaps off. A stronger rod beats stronger fish.
