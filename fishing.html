🎣 How To Fish

1
Watch the water — fish swim below the boat. Bigger + brighter = rarer.
2
Cast: hold to charge, release to drop your bait. More power = deeper + further.
3
Wait: a nearby fish will swim to your bait. When it bites, the bobber slams down — TAP!
4
Reel: HOLD to pull it up. The tension bar rises — release before it snaps or you lose the fish.
