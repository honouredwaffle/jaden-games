🎣 How To Fish

1
Watch the water — fish swim below the boat. Bigger + brighter = rarer.
2
Aim: at the boat, TAP the water where you want to cast — or DRAG your finger to slide the marker around. You can even aim behind the boat — the angler turns to face the way you're aiming. Further = rarer, deeper fish.
3
THROW: tap the THROW button (top-left) to cast to your marker. Tap the water again to re-aim before you throw.
4
Wait: a fish swims over and starts nibbling — the float dips. Usually best to WAIT, it circles and taps up to 3 times. Reel too early and it'll spook (sometimes it just backs off and stays).
5
Hook it: when it finally commits the float slams under — TAP anywhere (or REEL) to set the hook.
6
REEL: HOLD the REEL button to pull the fish up — the catch bar rises toward the surface. The fight starts in slow-mo so you've got time to react. Let go when the tension bar reddens.
7
Bar full = caught → it flies into your bucket. Bar empty = it snaps off. Then pick Keep (goes into your 🎒 crate, top-right — sell them any time) or Sell (straight to coins).
