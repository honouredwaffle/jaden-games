🎣 How To Fish

1
Cast: hold the screen to charge, release to cast. Land it near a fish you can see in the water.
2
Wait: a nearby fish will swim over. When the bobber yanks under — that's a BITE!
3
Hook: tap fast to set the hook. Don't miss the window.
4
Reel: hold to reel in. The tension bar rises — let go before it goes red or the line snaps. Keep the fish marker green!
